# papers I read on growing plants in regolith

quick takeaways so I can sanity check my ranking against what actually happened
when people grew plants.

- Russell 2022 (OSD-670): grew lettuce, radish and pepper in CI asteroid simulant
  mixed with peat. more simulant meant worse growth, and radish was hit hardest.
  they blamed compaction and crusting, not only the nutrients.
- Wamelink 2014: plants did germinate in diluted Mars and Moon simulant but grew
  poorly overall.
- Eichler 2021: unamended Martian simulant supported basically no growth.
- Duri 2022 (review): lunar simulants are alkaline with low CEC. JSC-1A gives poor
  biomass and germination, while LHS-1 can sustain a little growth without added
  fertilizer.
